Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to CW Club Spotter ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Call Sign: Your amateur radio call sign for filtering purposes
  • Filter Preferences: Your selected clubs, bands, speed ranges, and other filter settings

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, including frequency of use and feature utilization
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Network Data: API requests to RBN (Reverse Beacon Network) services for spot data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide personalized CW spot filtering based on your preferences
  • Maintain your filter settings across app sessions
  • Improve app performance and user experience
  • Display relevant amateur radio contest and event information

4. Data Storage

Your filter preferences and call sign are stored locally on your device using iOS UserDefaults. This data is not transmitted to our servers or any third parties except as necessary to fetch spot data from RBN services.

5. Third-Party Services

This app uses the following third-party services:

  • RBN (rbn.telegraphy.de): For real-time CW spot data. Please review their privacy policy at their website.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties. Your data is used solely for the functionality of the CW Club Spotter app.

7. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information. All data is stored locally on your device, and network communications use standard HTTPS encryption.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete all stored data by uninstalling the app
  • Reset your preferences using the "Reset" button in the app
  • Modify your call sign and filter settings at any time

9. Children's Privacy

This app is designed for amateur radio operators and is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app and on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
