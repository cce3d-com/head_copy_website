CW Club Spotter

Live CW Club Activity Tracker

Introduction

CW Club Spotter is your gateway to the global CW community. This app provides real-time monitoring of CW (Morse code) activity from the Reverse Beacon Network (RBN), allowing you to track club members, monitor contests, and discover active CW operators worldwide.

Whether you're a contest operator seeking competition insights, a club member looking for fellow operators, or a CW enthusiast exploring band activity, CW Club Spotter delivers the live data you need in an intuitive, easy-to-use interface.

Real-Time Tracking

Live spots updated every 30 seconds to 3 minutes, showing active CW operators as they're spotted by the global RBN network.

34+ CW Clubs

Monitor activity from major clubs including CWOPS, FOC, FISTS, HSC groups, SKCC, NAQCC, and many more.

Advanced Filtering

Customize your view by bands, clubs, speed ranges, geographic regions, and personal preferences.

iPad Optimized

Designed for both iPhone and iPad with optimized layouts that make the most of your device's screen space.

Getting Started

  1. Launch the app - CW Club Spotter will immediately begin loading live spots from the RBN network.
  2. Set your callsign - Go to Filters → Settings → Own Call and enter your amateur radio callsign to personalize your experience.
  3. Configure filters - Select the clubs, bands, and speed ranges you're interested in monitoring.
  4. Adjust refresh rate - Choose how often you want new data (30s-180s) based on your needs and data usage preferences.
  5. Start monitoring - Return to Club Spots to see your customized live activity feed.

App Overview

CW Club Spotter is organized into five main sections, accessible via the tab bar at the bottom:

📡 Club Spots

The main screen showing live CW spots. Each entry displays:

  • Callsign - The spotted operator's call sign
  • Frequency - Operating frequency in MHz
  • Speed - CW speed in WPM (words per minute)
  • Clubs - Associated club memberships (color-coded)
  • Age - How long ago the spot was reported
  • Spotters - Number of RBN stations that heard this signal
Tip: Recent spots (age ≤ 1 minute) are highlighted with a pink background. Tap "Show details" for complete spotter information and signal strength data.

🔧 Filters

Your command center for customizing what spots you see:

Clubs Section

  • All Clubs - Master toggle to enable/disable all club filters at once
  • Individual Clubs - Select specific clubs you want to monitor

Bands Section

  • All Bands - Master toggle for all amateur bands
  • Individual Bands - From 160m through 6m

Speed Ranges

Filter by CW sending speed from < 10 WPM to > 39 WPM

Skimmers From

Choose geographic regions: EU, NA, AS, SA, AF, OC

Settings

  • Refresh Rate - How often to fetch new data (30-180 seconds)
  • Sort By - Order spots by Frequency, Call, Age, Club, Speed, or Spotter
  • Max Age - Only show spots within this time limit (5-60 minutes)
  • Call Filter - Use wildcards to filter callsigns (e.g., "W1*" for W1 calls)
  • Own Call - Your callsign for personalized highlighting
  • Show Self Spots - Include or exclude spots of your own callsign
Note: Changes to filters automatically refresh the Club Spots data. More restrictive filters may result in fewer spots being displayed.

Tips for Effective Use

Contest Monitoring

During contests, set a fast refresh rate (30s) and filter by specific contest-active clubs. Sort by "Age" to see the most recent activity first.

Band Monitoring

Focus on specific bands by using the "All Bands" toggle to quickly disable everything, then enable only your bands of interest.

Club Activity

To monitor your club's activity, disable "All Clubs" and select only your specific club. This gives you a focused view of fellow members.

Data Usage

If concerned about mobile data usage, increase the refresh rate to 180 seconds and use more restrictive filters to reduce network requests.

Troubleshooting

No Spots Appearing

  • Check your internet connection
  • Verify that some filters are enabled (at least one club and one band)
  • Increase the "Max Age" setting to see older spots
  • Pull down on the Club Spots list to manually refresh

App Running Slowly

  • Increase the refresh rate to reduce network requests
  • Use more restrictive filters to reduce data processing
  • Restart the app if performance degrades over time

Filters Not Working

  • Tap the "Reset" button in Filters to restore default settings
  • Ensure at least one option is selected in each filter category
  • Check that "Max Age" isn't set too restrictively

Understanding RBN Data

The Reverse Beacon Network (RBN) is a network of automated listening stations that detect and report CW signals worldwide. Key points to understand:

  • Coverage Varies - Some bands and regions have better RBN coverage than others
  • Automatic Detection - RBN stations use software to decode CW, so very weak or malformed signals may not be spotted
  • No QSO Data - RBN only reports that a signal was heard, not complete QSO information
  • Speed Accuracy - WPM measurements are estimates based on the detected CW timing
  • Club Information - Club affiliations are based on databases and may not always be current
